    About

    Vlad and Artiom are a composer and sound design duo based in Saint Petersburg. We write for film, series, stage and live shows, from intimate piano to dark electronic and orchestral. We record live musicians in the studio and remotely, and work with directors on set or over video. English, French and Russian.
